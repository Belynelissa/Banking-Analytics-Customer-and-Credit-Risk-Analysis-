# Banking Analytics | Customer Value & Credit Risk

Client-facing SQL analytics work presented to paying clients, examining how customer profiles, account balances, transaction behaviour and loan records can be connected to support banking decisions.

The public examples use simulated data to protect confidential client information.

## Business questions explored

- Which customer segments hold the highest account balances?
- How do customer location and income segments vary?
- Which transaction channels have the highest usage and average transaction values?
- How is loan exposure distributed across loan statuses?
- Where are defaulted loans concentrated by income segment and loan size?
- Which customer-level indicators may warrant further review?

## What this work demonstrates

- Relational data modelling and SQL database setup.
- Data loading and staging in MySQL.
- Customer segmentation and balance-based deciles.
- Transaction channel and behaviour analysis.
- Loan portfolio monitoring and exploratory credit-risk analysis.
- Multi-table joins, CTEs, aggregations, conditional logic and window functions.

## Business relevance

The analysis provides a structured view of customer value, channel activity and lending exposure. It can support customer prioritisation, channel reviews and focused credit-risk investigation. It does not claim measured savings, revenue growth or reduced defaults; those outcomes require institution-specific validation and action.
